Honey Never Spoils

Archaeologists have found pots of honey in ancient Egyptian tombs that are over 3,000 years old and still completely edible due to its low moisture and natural acidity.

A Growing Tower

Because iron expands in the heat, the Eiffel Tower can grow up to 15 centimeters taller during hot summer days in Paris.
